Frequently asked questions

Do we have to be local to work with you?

No. About 80% of our engagements are fully remote. Consultations run on Teams, Zoom or Google Meet, whichever you use.

How is pricing structured?

Consulting and software projects are fixed-price per phase. Training is priced per seat with team discounts from 5 seats. Managed services are a monthly plan.

What do participants get access to after enrolling?

A portal login with the course schedule, recordings, downloadable materials, assignments and a direct message line to their trainer.

Can we request support after a project ends?

Yes. Submit a service request in the portal; you'll see its status and who's handling it, and you'll get an email and portal notification when it's resolved.
